MERCHFLUX — CHRISTMAS 2026 DESIGN PROMPTS
==========================================
12 trending POD niches · best-selling design concept · copy-paste prompt
Compiled 9 November 2026 · for Merch on Demand, KDP and Etsy

HOW TO RUN THESE
----------------
1. Append the aspect ratio: --ar 4:5 for Midjourney, or say "vertical 4:5" in plain-English
   tools. Ornament/monogram designs use --ar 1:1.
2. Generate the artwork, then set long text manually in Canva/Illustrator. Models still
   mangle any phrase longer than about four words.
3. Always ask for flat vector on pure white — never a mockup, a shirt, or a shadow.
4. Check the result at 200px wide. If you cannot read it there, it will not convert.
5. Nothing trademarked goes in. No characters, no lyrics, no celebrity names.

PRINT-FILE CHECKLIST (run before every upload)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] 4500 x 5400 px, 300 DPI, transparent PNG for apparel (remove the white ground)
[ ] Readable at 200 px wide in a search result grid
[ ] Maximum 3-4 colors — more looks muddy on a $17.99 tee
[ ] No character, lyric, team name or living person anywhere in the art or the title
[ ] Trademark check run on the exact title phrase, not the concept
[ ] Listed on the right product first: sweatshirt for women 25-55, tee for group buys
[ ] Price anchored at .99, raised 10-15% between 20 Nov and 12 Dec
